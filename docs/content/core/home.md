# Enterprise AI that already runs in production

**Quantum Atlas builds AI systems for enterprises, and runs each one in our own companies first.** No demos. No proofs of concept dressed up as products. If we sell it, it is already working somewhere we answer for.

[Book a discovery call](/contact) · [Talk to our voice agent](/services/ai-voice-agents)

## What is Quantum Atlas?

Quantum Atlas is an enterprise AI implementation studio. We design, build and run voice agents, document processing, knowledge search, custom software and automation for organisations that need them to work every day.

We are a separate brand from the logistics and water-treatment companies that use our systems, and we build for outside clients as well.

## What has actually been built?

**Direct answer:** Real systems doing real work. A tender radar scans 20 portals every day. A document pipeline reads shipping documents in about 8 seconds. A multi-tenant RAG platform runs for internal use. A voice agent answers questions on this site. Each fact below is from our own records.

| What | What it does | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Tender radar | Daily scan of 20 government portals, scored and posted as a digest | 54,526 tenders on the last full run, zero failures |
| Document pipeline | Reads shipping and finance documents with Google Document AI and LangGraph | About 8 s per 3-page document, down from about 120 s |
| Enterprise RAG | Multi-tenant knowledge search on Postgres with row-level security | Running for internal use; 80 automated tests |
| Voice agent | Real-time speech on self-hosted LiveKit with a knowledge tool | Live on this site; try it |
| Customs workflows | Bill-of-entry checklists, duty computation, ICEGATE-format file generation | Built into our logistics software |
| Cargofit | 3D container load planner | Live; one example loads 144 pieces at 74.4% fill |

## Why do we run everything in production first?

**Direct answer:** Because a demo hides the hard parts. Permissions, failures, cost, monitoring and handover only show up when something runs every day. We run our systems in our own companies, learn what breaks, then offer them to you with those lessons built in.

It also keeps us honest. When a capability is not proven, we say so. Our voice agent runs live on this site, and we run phone calling on an Indian carrier, but we publish no call volumes we have not measured. You will hear the same plain answer on a call.

## How is Quantum Atlas different?

**Direct answer:** Self-hosted by default, open components, and no proprietary lock-in. Your data, your code and your infrastructure stay yours. We choose Postgres over proprietary vector databases and LiveKit over hosted voice platforms, and we tell you what each choice costs.

| Question | Typical AI vendor | Quantum Atlas |

| Where does your data live? | Vendor cloud | Your infrastructure or ours, by agreement |

## How does an engagement work?

**Direct answer:** We plan before we build. You get a written scope, working software in phases and a sign-off at each step. We only promise what is built and tested.

1. **Discovery call.** A real conversation with the people who will build it.
2. **Written scope.** What we will build, what we will not, and how we will measure it.
3. **Pilot.** The narrowest useful version, running on your data.
4. **Review.** Every wrong result examined with you.
5. **Rollout and handover.** Monitoring, documentation and your ownership.

## Who do we build for?

**Direct answer:** Organisations where AI must run reliably every day: logistics and shipping, government and regulated bodies, finance and insurance, legal teams, manufacturers and healthcare. Our roots are in logistics, and our group companies have relationships across that sector.

We are not a fit for teams who want a quick chatbot, a throwaway demo or the lowest quote.

## Where does it run?

Our own fleet runs on six hosts behind Caddy, with Prometheus and Grafana for monitoring. We use the same pattern for client deployments: containers, Postgres, a reverse proxy and dashboards you can read.

## Frequently asked questions

### What does Quantum Atlas do?

We build and run enterprise AI systems: voice agents, document extraction, knowledge search over your documents, custom multi-tenant software and automated workflows. Everything is self-hosted or deployable on your infrastructure, and each system runs in our own companies before we offer it.

### Can I see a working system before I speak to you?

Yes. A voice agent is live on this site. Open the chat widget and talk to it. It runs on our own LiveKit server and answers from a knowledge base. It is a real deployment, and its limits are visible.

### Do you work on-premise?

Yes, for components that can run there: the database, retrieval, workflow engine and application code. Some models and APIs need outside access unless you host models yourself. We list every external dependency in the scope so your security team can review it.

### Which industries do you serve?

Logistics and shipping, government, banking and insurance, legal, manufacturing and healthcare. Our deepest production experience is in logistics, where our group companies operate.

### What does a project cost?

Pricing depends on scope, so we do not publish rates. We quote after a discovery call and a written scope, and we anchor on what the work saves you compared with today. We do not compete on the lowest price.

### Who owns the code and data?

You do. We deliver source code, database contents and deployment files, and third-party accounts are opened in your name. We aim for a system your own engineers can run without us.

### How do I start?

Book a discovery call. It is a conversation, not a form. Tell us the problem; we will tell you plainly whether we can solve it and what we would build first.
