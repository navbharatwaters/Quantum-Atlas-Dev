# AI content and video production, as a supporting service

## What is AI content and video production?

AI content and video production uses generative models to make or enhance images, avatars and video, then a human editor finishes the result. It suits explainer clips, training material and product demonstrations where a full film shoot is not justified.

## Why do we offer it?

**Direct answer:** Enterprise clients with a new AI system usually also need a training video, a demo reel or marketing assets. We offer content work so you do not need a separate agency for that. It supports our core services; it is not a standalone studio.

Our core work is [voice agents](/services/ai-voice-agents), [document intelligence](/services/document-intelligence), [enterprise RAG](/services/enterprise-rag), [SaaS development](/services/saas-development) and [automation](/services/ai-automation). Content is the smaller offering beside them, and we describe it that way.

## What can we make?

**Direct answer:** Short corporate videos, avatar-presented training clips, product demonstrations, image variations and upscaled or retouched assets. Output quality varies with the brief, the source material and the model, so we agree samples before a full run.

| Deliverable | Typical use | Our approach |
|---|---|---|
| Avatar-presented video | Training, onboarding, announcements | Digital avatar with lip-sync, scripted by you |
| Product demonstration | Sales and support | Screen capture, voice-over, edited in Premiere |
| Image generation and variation | Marketing assets | ComfyUI workflows with a defined style |
| Face-consistent assets | Brand ambassadors with consent | Identity-preserving workflows, only with written consent |
| Upscaling and inpainting | Repairing or enlarging existing media | ComfyUI-based enhancement |
| Longer generated clips | Short narrative or illustrative video | Segmented generation, then edited together |

Some of these are lab-grade capabilities. We show you the sample before you buy.

## How does a content project run?

**Direct answer:** Brief, sample, approval, production, finishing. We never produce a full batch before you approve a sample.

1. **Discovery call.** Audience, message, length and where it will be shown.
2. **Script and storyboard.** You approve the words and the shots.
3. **Sample.** A short test clip or image set in the proposed style.
4. **Production.** Generation in ComfyUI or the avatar tool, with human checks.
5. **Finishing.** Editing, captions, sound and colour in Adobe Premiere.
6. **Delivery.** Final files in the formats you need, plus the source project.

## How we deliver it

**Direct answer:** We build repeatable ComfyUI workflows for each style, so the second video matches the first. Humans review every output for errors, artefacts and anything that could mislead.

The tools in words:

- **ComfyUI** for node-based image and video generation, including lip-sync, upscaling and inpainting.
- **Avatar tools**, such as Tavus integration, for presenter-style video. [OWNER-CONFIRM: whether Tavus integration is live]
- **Adobe Premiere** for editing, captions and delivery formats.

Generation runs on GPU hardware. Which model and where it runs depends on the job and on licence terms.

## What you own

- **The final video and image files**, in the formats agreed.
- **The project files** for editing, where licences allow.
- **Scripts and storyboards.**
- **Workflow definitions** for repeating the style, if you ask for them.

Model and tool licences set limits on commercial use. We tell you which apply before we start.

## Does AI-generated content raise ethical issues?

**Direct answer:** Yes, and we handle them in the brief. We use a real person's face or voice only with written consent. We label synthetic presenters where the audience would expect to know. We do not make content designed to deceive.

This follows the same rule as the rest of our work: ethics is part of the technical decision, not a footnote.

## When it is not the right fit

- You need a broadcast-quality film with real actors and locations. Hire a production house.
- You want a convincing fake of a named person without their consent. We will not do it.
- You need hundreds of videos on a tight turnaround before a sample is approved. Quality comes first.
- You want content as your only engagement with us. We are better placed alongside a core service.
- You need guaranteed identical output across every run. Generative models vary, and we manage that with review.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is this a core service?

No. It is a supporting service. Our core work is voice agents, document intelligence, RAG, SaaS and automation. We offer content so you can keep to one vendor, and we scope it modestly to match.

### Can you make a video of our CEO without filming them?

Only with their written consent, and we would label the result as synthetic where viewers would expect to know. We will not create likenesses of anyone who has not agreed.

### How realistic are the avatars?

It varies with the model, the source footage and the script. We make a short sample first so you judge the real output, not a promise. Some uses suit an avatar, such as training clips, and some do not.

### What file formats do you deliver?

Standard video formats such as MP4, with captions, and image formats such as PNG and WebP. We confirm the formats in the brief. Source project files are included where licences permit.

### Can you localise a video into other languages?

Possibly. It depends on the speech and lip-sync models for the language. We test one sample in the target language before agreeing, and a native speaker should review the result.

### Who owns the generated content?

You own the delivered files as agreed in the contract. Model and tool licences can restrict commercial use, and copyright rules for generated work differ by country, so we flag this early and recommend legal review for important assets.

### How long does a video take?

It depends on length, complexity and revision rounds, so we quote after the script and sample. We would rather tell you honestly than promise a date we cannot hold.

### Can you demonstrate our software with video?

Yes. We capture the software, write the script, add a voice-over or avatar presenter and edit it. It pairs well with a [SaaS build](/services/saas-development) when you need a launch demo.
