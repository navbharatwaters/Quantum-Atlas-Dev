# Knowledge Hub

Plain-English material for people who have to decide whether, and how, to put AI into production. Everything here is written by the engineers who build these systems. We say what we have built, we say what we have not, and we do not publish numbers we cannot stand behind.

## What is in the hub?

The hub has four sections. Each one has a different job.

| Section | What it is | Who it is for | Gated? |
|---|---|---|---|
| Implementation guides | Checklists you can use in a meeting this week | Project owners, IT leads, operations heads | No |
| ROI calculator | A worksheet where you enter your own costs and volumes | Finance, operations, anyone building a business case | No |
| Whitepapers | Longer technical and procurement documents | Technical evaluators, legal teams, procurement officers | Email capture |
| Blog | Deep dives on systems we have built | Engineers, architects, technical buyers | No |

## Implementation guides

Short, practical checklists. Each is 700 to 1,000 words and ends with questions to ask of any vendor, including us.

- **Voice AI setup checklist.** What to settle before building a voice agent: use case, channels, latency, escalation, disclosure, testing.
- **RAG requirements.** What an enterprise knowledge system needs: sources, access rules, citations, audit, evaluation, deployment.
- **Document coverage guide for intelligent document processing.** How to inventory your document types and decide what to automate first.
- **AI readiness assessment.** A scored self-check across data, process, people, security and operations.

## ROI calculator

An interactive worksheet. You enter your own volumes and costs, including what you currently pay per document, per minute or per hour, and the calculator shows monthly and annual differences and a payback period. It does not come with built-in prices for any other vendor. If you want to compare with a competitor's published rate, you enter that rate yourself, so the comparison is yours and you can defend it. The full specification is in the ROI calculator specification.

## Whitepapers

Longer documents, available after you enter an email address. We use your address to send the document and, if you agree, occasional follow-ups. You can unsubscribe at any time.

- **Enterprise RAG on-premise.** An architecture and operations guide for running retrieval-augmented generation inside your own infrastructure.
- **Contract intelligence for legal teams.** How clause extraction, obligation tracking and question answering over a contract corpus work, and where human review stays essential.
- **Government AI procurement checklist.** Technical requirements and questions for tender documents and vendor evaluation.

## How we write

- Concrete over abstract. Real components, real constraints.
- Honest about limits. Every guide says what the approach does not do.
- No invented statistics. If a number appears, it is either yours (typed into the calculator) or sourced.
- No names of customers, and no claims of certifications.
